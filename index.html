OnlyFans management FAQ

Questions About Working With a US OnlyFans Agency?

What's the agency split?

It depends on what you need, and it's always agreed in writing before you sign. Chatting only: we take 25% if you make under $50K a month, and 20% once you're over $50K a month. Full management (marketing, chatting and daily account management): a 50/50 split.

Is there a lock-in contract?

No. Straton has no term contracts, so you can leave whenever you want. All we ask is that you tell us.

What happens after I apply?

You get a message from us in under a minute, and a real person on our team follows up. Our team answers around the clock. If it looks like a fit, we set up a strategy call about your page and your goals.

How do I get paid?

You earn from subscriptions, tips, pay-per-view content and paid messages. Payouts go from OnlyFans straight to your own bank account. Your account and your payouts stay in your name.

What's in it for me?

More earning potential with less of your time. We bring in new fans, answer every message 24/7 and price your content by what fans actually buy, while you focus on creating. On the strategy call we show you where we think your page can grow.

How long before I see results with OnlyFans management?

It depends on your starting point, your content and how consistently you post. Some things change as soon as your team goes live: fans get replies around the clock and PPV offers start going out. Growing new subscribers through marketing builds over weeks and months, not overnight. We'll talk through realistic goals for your page before you sign. No OnlyFans agency can honestly guarantee results, and we won't.

Can I do this privately?

Yes. Geo-blocking hides your page from your home state, our DMCA takedown service gets leaked content pulled down, only our team sees your application, and nothing posts without your OK. You decide what you show.

How do DMCA takedowns remove leaked OnlyFans content?

You own the copyright to your content. When it gets leaked, a DMCA takedown notice asks the site hosting it to remove it, and asks Google to drop those pages from search results. Sites that ignore valid notices risk losing their legal protection, so mainstream sites and Google usually act on them. When leaked content turns up, our DMCA takedown service files the notices for you, as part of Ghost Mode. Some sites hosted overseas ignore notices, so no one can promise every copy disappears, but takedowns help get leaks out of search results and off mainstream sites.

How big is the Straton team?

Straton is a team of 56+ people. You get a dedicated team on your account, plus direct access to Matvei, our Founder & CEO.

Is Straton a US OnlyFans agency?

Yes. Straton LLC is a US company headquartered in Los Angeles, California. Chatting runs 24/7, and top accounts get US-based chatters.

Will I keep ownership and control of my account?

Yes. You own your account and your content. You decide what you are comfortable creating, and your approval guides every major decision.

What does an OnlyFans management agency do?

An OnlyFans management agency runs the business side of your page. At Straton that means marketing and traffic, 24/7 fan chatting, pricing and PPV strategy, and daily account management. You focus on creating content and approve the plan.
