Only our team sees your application and account details, and your chatters work within the boundaries you set. Your account, content and payouts stay in your name.

Yes. You own your account and your content. You approve the strategy, protect your boundaries and remain in control.

Who will be communicating with my fans?

Trained chatters on a dedicated team, led by team leaders who review conversations every day. Real people, never AI bots. Our chatting team is based in the US and EU.

What happens after I apply?

You get a message from us in under a minute, and a real person on our team follows up. Our team answers around the clock. If it looks like a fit, we review your page and set up an introductory call.

How do I get paid?

You earn from subscriptions, tips, pay-per-view content and paid messages. Payouts go from OnlyFans straight to your own bank account. Your account and your payouts stay in your name.

What is the onboarding process like?

After the introductory call, your chatters learn your voice, boundaries and fan relationships, and we set up your strategy and workflow together before going live.
